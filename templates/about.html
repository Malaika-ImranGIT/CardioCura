About Our Facility

Learn and Explore our facility

Ambulance Service

At CardioCura, our Ambulance Service is available 24/7 to ensure timely medical assistance during emergencies. We are equipped with state-of-the-art ambulances staffed by trained paramedics and emergency medical technicians (EMTs) to provide immediate care and safe transport to our facility. Whether it’s a critical accident, a medical emergency at home, or a need for inter-hospital transfer, our fleet is ready to respond quickly and efficiently. With GPS-enabled tracking, advanced life support systems, and real-time communication with our emergency room, we ensure that every second counts.

Free Check-Ups

At CardioCura, we believe that prevention is better than cure. That's why we offer free health check-ups to promote early detection and encourage a healthier lifestyle for our community. Our complimentary check-up packages include basic screenings such as blood pressure, blood sugar, BMI, and general wellness assessments — all conducted by our experienced medical staff. These check-ups are designed to help you stay informed about your health, identify potential risks early, and take proactive steps toward well-being. Whether you're young or elderly, regular check-ups can make a significant difference. Take the first step toward a healthier life — because your health deserves attention, even when you feel fine.

Emergency Rooms

At CardioCura, our Emergency Rooms are open 24/7 to provide immediate care when every second matters. Staffed with experienced emergency physicians, nurses, and trauma specialists, we are equipped to handle a wide range of critical situations — from accidents and sudden illnesses to cardiac emergencies and more. Our facility features advanced diagnostic tools, life-saving equipment, and real-time monitoring systems to ensure rapid diagnosis and effective treatment. We follow streamlined protocols to reduce wait times and deliver timely, compassionate care when you need it most. In moments of crisis, trust MediCura Emergency Rooms — where your life is our highest priority.
